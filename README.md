# rhythm-pitch-continuum
Max/MSP demonstration of Henry Cowell's rhythm–pitch continuum, illustrating the transformation of rhythmic ratios into musical intervals.

Educational Max/MSP patch accompanying the "Conlon Nancarrow" session of the university course Sound Art and Technology.


This patch demonstrates Henry Cowell's concept that rhythmic ratios become musical intervals when accelerated into the audible frequency range. It accompanies the openly available teaching materials at teaching.medienkunst-sound.de.
