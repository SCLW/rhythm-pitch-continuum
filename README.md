Two click trains at rates *f* and *f·r*: at 2 Hz the ratio is a polyrhythm you can count; accelerated into the audible range, it becomes the musical interval (after Henry Cowell, *New Musical Resources*, 1930). Just ratios meet again every *q* beats — the irrational ones, √2 and π/e, share one downbeat and never meet again.

**Patch:** [`patch/demo1-rhythm-intervals.maxpat`](patch/demo1-rhythm-intervals.maxpat) — requires [Max](https://cycling74.com/downloads) 8+, which is free to download and runs patches without a license. Start the audio, click a ratio, drag the speed.
